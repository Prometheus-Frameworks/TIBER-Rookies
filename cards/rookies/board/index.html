How It Works
01
Multi-Signal Grading

Each rookie receives a composite Rookie Grade combining athletic testing (SPORQ/combine), college production, and draft capital into a single 0–100 score.

02
Tier Classification

Players are sorted into four tiers — Top Cluster, Strong Starter, Development, and Swing — so you can instantly see where talent cliffs occur in each class.

03
Compare & Shortlist

Use the compare tool for head-to-head breakdowns, then add players to your shortlist queue with custom tags and notes for draft-day decisions.

Tier Guide
Top Cluster
Grade ≥ 75

Franchise-caliber prospects with elite multi-signal profiles. Highest confidence, maximum upside.

Strong Starter
Grade 70–74

High-floor prospects projected as reliable NFL starters with strong collegiate evidence.

Development
Grade 65–69

Prospects with one or two above-average signals. Solid depth or eventual starter potential.

Swing Tier
Grade < 65

High-variance prospects or late-round bets. Check individual profiles for specific upside signals.
