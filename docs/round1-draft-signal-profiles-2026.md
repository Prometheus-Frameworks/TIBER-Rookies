# 2026 Round 1 Draft Signal Profiles

## Purpose

This artifact captures a lightweight, human-readable **post-draft interpretation layer** for 2026 Round 1 rookie skill players and QBs.

It is intentionally separate from the pre-draft TIBER prospect evaluation and from Rookie Alpha score generation.

- It freezes immediate draft-signal interpretation for translator calibration.
- It does **not** modify model scoring, ranking math, or promoted Rookie Alpha artifacts.
- It provides structured notes that can later be reconciled against canonical `draft_results` data artifacts.

## Core Doctrine: Split Draft Capital into Two Signals

Round 1 capital should be interpreted as two distinct signals:

1. **Talent Confirmation Signal**
   - How much draft position updates confidence in underlying player quality.
   - This is where class-wide inflation risk can reduce certainty.

2. **Opportunity Insulation Signal**
   - How much draft position updates confidence in role patience, usage runway, and organizational investment.
   - This can remain strong/elite even when talent confirmation is treated more cautiously.

Keeping these signals separate prevents a false binary where draft capital is treated as either fully trusted or fully discounted.

## Why This Is Separate from Pre-Draft Grade

Pre-draft TIBER stance reflects model and prospect evidence available before NFL selection outcomes.

Post-draft signal profiles are a translator-layer update describing how the NFL market outcome should adjust interpretation **without rewriting the pre-draft grade itself**.

This preserves traceability:

- Pre-draft model view remains auditable.
- Post-draft interpretation is explicit and reversible.
- Future calibration can evaluate which post-draft signal assumptions were directionally right or wrong.

## Class Inflation Handling

A class can have elevated Round 1 offensive-skill capital versus historical baselines.

When that happens:

- **Talent confirmation** may be downgraded from elite certainty to strong/mixed confidence for specific players.
- **Opportunity insulation** should still generally reflect real NFL investment and runway effects.

This avoids over-correcting in either direction:

- We do not blindly map all premium picks to identical talent certainty.
- We do not ignore the practical opportunity effects created by premium capital.

## Relationship to Future Outcome Calibration

These profiles are draft-week translator snapshots. They are not final truths.

In later cycles, they should connect to historical calibration work by comparing:

- declared talent-confirmation tier,
- declared opportunity-insulation tier,
- short-term runway expectation,
- and realized fantasy-role / production outcomes.

That feedback loop supports better translator priors while preserving pre-draft model integrity.

## Guardrails

- No Rookie Alpha scoring changes are made by this artifact.
- No promoted alpha outputs are regenerated by this artifact.
- No proprietary analyst content is embedded.
- No third-party scouting reports are cited or reproduced.
- Operator-provided Round 1 facts should be reconciled against canonical `draft_results` artifacts in subsequent pipeline steps.
